Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Arctic Flow Inc.

1. Overview

This policy explains how Arctic Flow Inc. ("Arctic Flow", "we") collects, uses and protects information when you use our website and apps. Your job records, client details and payment data belong to your business — we process them only to provide the Service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, company name and password (stored only as a cryptographic hash).
  • Business data: jobs, schedules, client records, invoices and extra charges you create.
  • Payment metadata: transaction status and identifiers from our payment providers. We never store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, IP address and diagnostic logs used to keep the Service secure and reliable.

3. How we use information

We use your information to operate the Service, sync schedules across your teams, process payments through providers, provide support, prevent fraud and improve features. We do not sell personal data or use your business data for advertising.

4. Sharing

We share data only with processors that help us run the Service — such as cloud hosting, payment processing and email delivery — under contracts that restrict their use of your data. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Access is role-based and logged, two-factor authentication is supported, and backups run daily. No system is perfectly secure, but we design to industry standards and review controls regularly.

6. Retention & deletion

You can export or delete your business data at any time from account settings. When you close an account, we delete or anonymize personal data within 90 days, except where retention is legally required.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (including GDPR and similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in-app or by email before they take effect.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Email privacy@arcticflow.app.
